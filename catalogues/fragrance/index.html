THE COMPOSITION

Warmth, in three movements.

A fictional fragrance composition expressed through the textures and tones of its imagined ingredients.

Cedarwood

AN EXERCISE IN RESTRAINT

Designed to be felt.
Not just seen.

VELLUNE is an imagined fragrance house, created as a study in material, typography and product storytelling. The hero bottle is built from real-time 3D geometry. Every face, edge and detail can be explored within the page.
