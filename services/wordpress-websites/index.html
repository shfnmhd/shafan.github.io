03 / WordPress websites

WordPress websites your team can actually maintain.

WordPress is a practical option when a business needs to publish and update its own content. I approach WordPress projects as websites with both a public experience and an editing experience. The aim is to create a structure that looks considered on the frontend while making routine changes straightforward for the people maintaining it.

Use WordPress when it fits the content workflow.

WordPress can make sense for a company website, a service-led business, an editorial site, a portfolio, or a catalogue where administrators regularly change information. It is not automatically the right answer for every application. A specialised workflow or product system may need a different platform.

Before recommending the setup, I look at who will edit the website, what types of content they need to change, and how often the site will be updated. A company with recurring articles has different requirements from a business that only changes service pages occasionally. Those requirements determine the structure of the editing interface, page templates and navigation.

A design system, not a collection of unrelated pages.

A well-structured WordPress site uses repeatable components. If staff add a new service, article, testimonial or catalogue entry, the existing design should provide consistent spacing, typography and image treatment without requiring someone to recreate the page by hand.

The development can include a tailored theme or an agreed theme foundation, reusable content layouts, navigation, and content fields. I prefer a measured plugin selection that addresses specific needs rather than adding multiple tools that overlap. The agreed layout also takes account of different content lengths and image proportions so that common edits do not break the design.

  • Responsive layouts, reusable page patterns and consistent styles.
  • Content organisation for pages, articles or agreed custom content types.
  • Editing fields and permissions aligned with the staff responsible for changes.
  • Image sizing guidance and logical media organisation.
  • Search engine metadata and crawlable content structure.
  • A basic handover covering the agreed editing and maintenance tasks.

Before starting a new site or redesign.

For a new website, I need the brand materials, page requirements, the content or a content plan, and relevant photography or product information. For an existing WordPress site, the project begins with understanding the theme, hosting, installed plugins, current content and important workflows. Read-only access or a suitable staging copy may be enough for the initial review.

If the existing site already receives visitors or has pages indexed by search engines, a redesign should consider existing page URLs and redirects rather than simply deleting or renaming everything. Domain and business email configurations are separate from the website itself and should not be modified unnecessarily during a site migration.

Build with a sensible maintenance plan.

WordPress websites require maintenance. Core software, themes and plugins need updates, and the hosting arrangement should include an appropriate backup and recovery process. I plan for the expected administration workflow rather than presenting updates as a task that happens automatically without a responsible person.

Before launch, I review the pages and common editing activities, check responsiveness, validate agreed forms and test navigation. Basic security-conscious configuration includes sensible account permissions, reducing unnecessary plugins, and avoiding the publication of private configuration values. No website is immune to security issues, so hosting, updates and ongoing maintenance responsibilities need a clear owner.

Know what is and is not part of development.

Paid theme or plugin licences, hosting subscriptions, domain purchases, copywriting, ecommerce processing, advanced integrations and ongoing maintenance are separate items unless specifically agreed. A redesign also does not automatically include a full content migration if the amount and condition of the existing data have not been established.
